Combined routes

Elite institutions are measured repeatedly—and their routes do not quote the same margin.

Each row is a university. The diamond is its seat-weighted estimate; coloured dots are the separate exam or recommendation readings. A long connector is a real warning about route mix, not uncertainty hidden behind a single league-table rank.
